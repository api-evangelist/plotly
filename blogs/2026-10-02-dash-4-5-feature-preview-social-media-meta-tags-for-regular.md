New in Dash v4.5.0 Dash Pages automatically adds social media meta tags based on the title , description , and image for each page. Dash v4.5 extends this support to regular Dash apps using app-level metadata. Social media platforms such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Discord, and others use Open Graph and Twitter meta tags to generate a preview when someone shares a link.
