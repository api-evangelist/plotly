Hey everyone - Really excited to share this feature that we launched today: Plotly Studio Embedded. If you’ve been following my work over the last year, you may know that I’ve been heads down in building out Plotly Studio (Desktop): our take on what AI-enhanced data analytics and Dash app development that puts the data front-and-center. Plotly Studio Embedded takes the core ideas and architecture from that product and makes it available on top of your Dash app.
